Technology doesn’t scale through hype.

It scales through infrastructure.

But most people only see the hype phase.

* * *

### **The Hype Phase**

Every new technology starts with:

-   excitement
-   speculation
-   attention

This creates:

👉 rapid growth

But it’s unstable.

* * *

### **The Drop-Off**

After hype:

-   attention fades
-   interest declines
-   weak projects disappear

This phase is often misinterpreted as failure.

* * *

### **The Infrastructure Phase**

Real progress happens quietly:

-   systems improve
-   adoption stabilizes
-   integration increases

This is where technology becomes:

👉 usable

This is where technology shifts from visibility to [real-world impact](https://jasonansell.ca/?p=3667).

* * *

### **Why Most People Miss It**

Because infrastructure isn’t exciting.

It doesn’t trend.

But it matters more than hype.

This is also where [systems begin to form and scale](https://jasonansell.ca/?p=3657).

* * *

### **The Pattern Repeats**

Every cycle:

-   hype → attention
-   drop → correction
-   infrastructure → growth

* * *

### **WTF does it all mean?**

Hype creates visibility.

Infrastructure creates value.

The people who understand the difference  
position early.
