# A GUIDE: Swapping tokens on Raydium

Swapping tokens on Raydium is similar to other platforms, just faster and cheaper. How to Swap 1.Navigate to Swap using the tabs at the of the app, then connect your wallet. 2.Next, select from the drop-down the tokens you want to swap. For this example, we’ll be swapping From SOL To USDC. ...

Swapping tokens on Raydium is similar to other platforms, just faster and cheaper. 

**How to Swap**

![](https://i0.wp.com/711343548-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MT-qzD26DX_h6l_vXaA-887967055%2Fuploads%2FNttJFp6hIGp7KTE2vwWX%2FScreen%20Shot%202022-10-01%20at%2015.49.28.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

1.  1.Navigate to Swap using the tabs at the of the app, then connect [your wallet](https://docs.raydium.io/raydium/trading-on-serum/spl-wallets).
2.  2.Next, select from the drop-down the tokens you want to swap. For this example, we’ll be swapping **From** SOL **To** USDC.

![](https://i0.wp.com/711343548-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MT-qzD26DX_h6l_vXaA-887967055%2Fuploads%2F3l4IMVjsstLq88NXp28y%2FScreen%20Shot%202022-10-01%20at%2015.53.45.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

3\. Enter the amount of tokens you wish to swap. Finally, click Swap!

![](https://i0.wp.com/711343548-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MT-qzD26DX_h6l_vXaA-887967055%2Fuploads%2FIhzvbS2Q7RPXCUP7X4pl%2Fimage.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

4\. Shortly after the transaction is confirmed you should be able to see your new balance, although this can sometimes take a few seconds to refresh.

You have now done your first swap on Raydium. Now let’s get swapping!

![](https://i0.wp.com/711343548-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MT-qzD26DX_h6l_vXaA-887967055%2Fuploads%2FJq2eCF5M7HbDsC4XrSVW%2FScreen%20Shot%202022-10-01%20at%2016.04.42.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Pay close attention to Price Impact and remember to check ‘Minimum Received’ when executing large swaps or when swapping in a low liquidity pool.

**Minimum Received** is the least amount of tokens a user will receive on a trade.

**Price Impact** is the difference between the market price and the estimated execution price based on the size of the trade.

**Slippage Tolerance** is the maximum difference between a user’s estimated price and execution price. You can access this parameter both by clicking “settings” on the navigation tab and “more information” on the swap interface.

![](https://i0.wp.com/711343548-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-MT-qzD26DX_h6l_vXaA-887967055%2Fuploads%2F3WTwecEMzzIqOyY8SHRi%2FScreen%20Shot%202022-10-01%20at%2015.58.06.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Now that you know how to use raydium you can easily swap sol, usdc or many other tokens.

## Key takeaways

- Connect your wallet to the Raydium app to begin trading tokens.
- Select your preferred input and output tokens and enter the transaction amount.
- Monitor price impact and minimum received values before confirming your token swap.
- Adjust slippage tolerance settings to manage potential differences between estimated and execution prices.
