For years, the focus was on net worth.

Assets.  
Equity.  
Long-term growth.

But in today’s environment, something else matters more.

Cash flow.

### The Problem With Paper Wealth

You can have a high net worth and still struggle financially.

Because:

-   Assets aren’t always liquid
-   Markets fluctuate
-   Access to capital isn’t guaranteed

Net worth looks good on paper.  
Cash flow keeps you stable.

### Why Cash Flow Matters More Now

With uncertainty in the economy:

-   Costs are rising
-   Income stability is less certain
-   Liquidity matters more

Having consistent income gives you flexibility.

### Building Modern Income Streams

Today, cash flow comes from:

-   Digital businesses
-   Affiliate income
-   Content monetization
-   Crypto (staking, yields — carefully)

The goal isn’t just wealth — it’s income.

### The Mindset Shift

Instead of asking:  
“How much am I worth?”

More people are asking:  
“How much do I generate monthly?”

That’s a different game.

### WTF does it all mean?

Net worth is important — but it doesn’t pay your bills.

Cash flow does.

And in uncertain times, income beats potential.
