## Why I Wrote _The Dark Side of Web3_

Web3 promised a lot of powerful ideas.

-   Financial freedom
-   Ownership of digital assets
-   Permissionless innovation
-   A more open internet

But those same features also created the **perfect environment for scams**.

In traditional finance, there are layers of protection.

Banks can reverse transactions.  
Customer support teams can intervene.  
Fraud departments can step in.

In Web3, **transactions are final**.

Once funds leave your wallet, they’re usually gone.

That’s why education is so important.

The book was written to help people understand **how scams actually work**, not just tell horror stories about them.

## Most Crypto Scams Follow the Same Pattern

One of the biggest misconceptions in the crypto space is that scams are random or unpredictable.

In reality, most scams follow the **same psychological playbook**.

They rely on a few common triggers:

-   urgency
-   social proof
-   fear of missing out
-   impersonation of authority
-   complex explanations that hide simple deception

Once you understand these patterns, spotting scams becomes much easier.

The book breaks down the anatomy behind some of the most common attacks in Web3.
