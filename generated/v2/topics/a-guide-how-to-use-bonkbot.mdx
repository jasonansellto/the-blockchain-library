##### Initializing BONKbot

Search by username for **Bonkbot\_bot**, or use this [telegram link](https://t.me/bonkbot_bot?start=ref_c7ikn).

Press start or type /start and you will be greeted by the following message.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2F7MCVWigM3pJnhqEw1j3O%2Fimage.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Great! You have now initialized an empty wallet. You will be buying and selling from here.

##### Depositing Sol

At this stage, you can send a small amount of Sol to the address from any wallet you use.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2Fe4O02CrjcJ9VSZTdxzsO%2Fimage.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

**You can tap on the link to copy it.**

Then, use your wallet of choice – Phantom, Backpack – and transfer yourself some Sol to start trading!

##### Buying Tokens

Type /start again, and your wallet should refresh with the balance you sent.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2FR54HtwKQtAz6HPGweXFY%2FScreenshot%202023-08-17%20at%2017.58.42.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

You can now buy VRSOL tokens using the amount you deposited, by pressing the Buy button.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2FuzWKqlsr0Ot3WQpnsnAK%2FScreenshot%202023-08-17%20at%2017.59.31.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Reply with the $VRSOL token address, or name VRSOL, and you can choose how much of the token you want to buy.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2FCESsJpUTKP1FL89Pxdfq%2FScreenshot%202023-08-17%20at%2018.01.43.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Press Buy X Sol, and you can choose how much you would like to buy.

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2FzrDaovBKLs1uSf9G6LWf%2FScreenshot%202023-08-17%20at%2018.04.14.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

Watch as your transaction is executed instantly

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2F3OC9yqlgFa73cPIodkkq%2FScreenshot%202023-08-17%20at%2018.04.34.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

**Congratulations! You just bought your first VRSOL tokens on BONKbot.**

##### Managing your positions

Having successfully bought a token, you can now manage your position by clicking Manage Positions

Bonkbot has specially curated a selection of data that helps you be a better trader, including recent price history so you can understand trends.

You can see:

-   Current Profit, both as a percentage and absolute
-   Current Value, in USD and Sol
-   The Market Cap of the token you bought
-   Price Changes in the last 5 mins, 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours
-   Net Profit, after subtracting Price Impact, DEX fees and a 1% BONKbot fee
-   Initial Value
-   Token Balance
-   Profit with Slippage
-   Wallet Balance

![](https://i0.wp.com/3681461756-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F0eLFJtXfMfGO6oK7Pm2J%2Fuploads%2Fh5vp2Fn0Dzd0BdvfXCzt%2FCapture%20d%E2%80%99e%CC%81cran%202023-12-22%20a%CC%80%2017.05.18.png?w=1140&ssl=1)

##### Selling Tokens

Selling your tokens is just as fast as buying them! On the Manage Positions menu, you will be able to view your account on Solscan and the token’s chart on Birdeye.

If you have multiple positions, you can cycle through them with the arrows, and you can choose to buy more, or sell some or all of your position.

![](https://i0.wp.com/vsgofficial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image.png?w=1140&ssl=1)
