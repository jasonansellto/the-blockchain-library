## 💡 What Is Listen-to-Earn?

**Listen-to-Earn** is VSC Radio’s latest Web3 innovation — a system that turns your time, attention, and community engagement into **real crypto rewards**.

No investments. No staking.  
Just music, interaction, and rewards.

By listening to VSC Radio, chatting with other listeners, voting in polls, and joining live DJ events, you earn **points** that can later be redeemed for **$PLUR tokens** — the official VSC Radio community token on the **Vector Smart Chain (VSC)**.

### 1️⃣ Earn Points

You earn points automatically while you listen to the radio, join chat discussions, vote in polls, or take part in live events.

### 2️⃣ Accumulate

### 3️⃣ Redeem for $PLUR

Once you reach **500,000 points**, you can redeem them for **real $PLUR tokens** sent directly to your verified crypto wallet.

## 💵 Redemption System

**1 Point = 1 PLUR Token**

### Redemption Tiers

-   🥉 **500,000 Points** → 500,000 PLUR
-   🥈 **1,000,000 Points** → 1,000,000 PLUR
-   🥇 **2,500,000 Points** → 2,500,000 PLUR
-   💎 **5,000,000 Points** → 5,000,000 PLUR

🔒 **Minimum Redemption:** 500,000 points (Entry Tier)

All redemptions are manually reviewed by admins for **security and fairness** before tokens are sent to your verified wallet.

* * *

## 🔗 How to Redeem

1.  **Connect & Verify Wallet**  
    Link your MetaMask or VSC Network wallet. Verify ownership by signing a message to ensure your tokens go to the correct address.
2.  **Accumulate Points**  
    Keep listening, chatting, and participating — your dashboard shows your total points.
3.  **Submit Redemption Request**  
    Once you hit 500,000+ points, head to your profile and request redemption.
4.  **Admin Approval & Transfer**  
    After review, tokens are sent directly to your wallet with a blockchain transaction ID for transparency.
