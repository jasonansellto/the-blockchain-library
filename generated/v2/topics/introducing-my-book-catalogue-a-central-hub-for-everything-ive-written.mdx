## Why I Created This

One of the biggest problems in crypto isn’t access to information.

It’s clarity.

There’s no shortage of content — but most of it is:

-   Overcomplicated
-   Overhyped
-   Or trying to sell something

When I started writing, the goal was simple:

No unnecessary jargon.  
No hype cycles.  
No assumptions about prior knowledge.

Just clear explanations of how things work and how to think about them.

This catalogue brings all of that together in one place.
