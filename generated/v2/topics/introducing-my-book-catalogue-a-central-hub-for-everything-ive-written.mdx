## Why I Created This

One of the biggest problems in crypto isn’t access to information.

It’s clarity.

There’s no shortage of content — but most of it is:

-   Overcomplicated
-   Overhyped
-   Or trying to sell something

When I started writing, the goal was simple:

Break things down in a way that actually makes sense.

No unnecessary jargon.  
No hype cycles.  
No assumptions about prior knowledge.

Just clear explanations of how things work and how to think about them.

This catalogue brings all of that together in one place.

## What You’ll Find in the Catalogue

The site includes my full collection of books, including:

-   Foundational guides to blockchain and cryptocurrency
-   Deep dives into Web3 and decentralized systems
-   Practical breakdowns of DeFi and digital assets
-   Security-focused content like protecting seed phrases
-   Real-world perspectives on scams and risk

Whether someone is just getting started or trying to sharpen their understanding, everything is organized to make it easy to navigate.
