# The Infinite Machine

This book chronicles the founding and development of Ethereum, detailing the vision of Vitalik Buterin and the internal conflicts of the early team. It is an essential read for understanding the platform that enabled smart contracts and the DeFi movement.

**How an Army of Crypto-hackers Is Building the Next Internet with Ethereum**

This book chronicles the founding and development of Ethereum, detailing the vision of Vitalik Buterin and the internal conflicts of the early team. It is an essential read for understanding the platform that enabled smart contracts and the DeFi movement.
