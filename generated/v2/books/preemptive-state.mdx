# Preemptive State

A dark philosophical sci-fi thriller exploring predictive surveillance, AI-driven governance, digital identity systems, and the quiet normalization of algorithmic control. Preemptive State expands the Protocol Zero universe into a world where predictive systems no longer simply observe behavior — they actively intervene before deviation occurs. Governments, corporations, and automated infrastructure operate through layers of predictive analytics capable of identifying risk before action takes place. Citizens are scored. Behavior is monitored continuously. Correction becomes automated. As individuals begin disappearing from the edges of society, questions emerge about who still controls the system.
