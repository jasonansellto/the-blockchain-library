# The Final Consensus

A philosophical sci-fi thriller exploring artificial intelligence, algorithmic governance, surveillance systems, and the quiet collapse of human autonomy in a hyperconnected society. Set in a near-future society where predictive systems quietly shape every aspect of life, the novel follows the growing realization that society's most important decisions are no longer being made by people. Invisible systems monitor behavior. Algorithms influence outcomes. Prediction becomes policy. As citizens become increasingly dependent on automated infrastructure and digital governance, resistance itself begins to feel statistically impossible.
