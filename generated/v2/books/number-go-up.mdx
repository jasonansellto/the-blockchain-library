# Number Go Up

An investigative journalist's global journey through the most eccentric and controversial corners of the crypto world during the 2021 bull run and 2022 crash. It provides a critical look at the rise of Sam Bankman-Fried, the mystery of Tether, and the impact of speculative mania on everyday people.
