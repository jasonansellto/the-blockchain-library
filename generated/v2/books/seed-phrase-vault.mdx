# Seed Phrase Vault

A professionally designed crypto journal and seed phrase log book to help cryptocurrency holders safely organize and store wallet recovery phrases, account details, and security notes offline. As cryptocurrency adoption continues to grow, properly protecting access to wallets has become one of the most important responsibilities for crypto holders. Losing a recovery phrase can result in permanent loss of access to digital assets. This journal provides a simple and organized system for securely recording seed phrases, wallet names, exchange information, recovery details, backup notes, and security reminders. Unlike storing sensitive information digitally, an offline written backup helps reduce exposure to hacking, phishing, malware, and device failure.
