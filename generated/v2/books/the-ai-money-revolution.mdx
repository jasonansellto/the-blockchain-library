# The AI Money Revolution

A practical examination of how artificial intelligence, Bitcoin, stablecoins, programmable payments, tokenized assets, and autonomous economic agents could reshape the global economy.

**How Artificial Intelligence, Bitcoin, and Digital Assets Are Rebuilding the Global Economy**

What happens when artificial intelligence can earn money, make purchases, negotiate agreements, and participate directly in global markets?

Artificial intelligence is rapidly becoming capable of performing work, making decisions, using tools, creating products, and coordinating complex activities. Yet the financial system was designed for people, companies, banks, and governments—not autonomous software.

The AI Money Revolution explores what happens when machine intelligence converges with Bitcoin, stablecoins, programmable payments, tokenized assets, and continuously operating digital markets.

This is not a book of speculative cryptocurrency price predictions. It is a practical examination of the infrastructure, opportunities, risks, and policy decisions that may define the next stage of the global economy.
