# The Dark Side of Web3

A practical guide to recognizing fraud, manipulation, and dangerous projects in the crypto industry while learning how to safely navigate Web3. As blockchain technology continues to grow, so do the number of scams targeting new users entering the space. This book explores common cryptocurrency scams, rug pulls and fraudulent projects, fake investment schemes, wallet theft and phishing attacks, social engineering tactics, NFT scams and manipulation, influencer-driven hype cycles, the psychology behind online fraud, red flags users should recognize, and practical security strategies for protecting digital assets.
