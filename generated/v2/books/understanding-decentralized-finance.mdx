# Understanding Decentralized Finance

A beginner-friendly guide designed to help readers understand how blockchain-powered financial systems work without relying on traditional banks or centralized institutions. This book explains what DeFi is, how decentralized finance works, the role of blockchain technology in financial systems, what smart contracts do, how decentralized exchanges function, staking and yield farming basics, liquidity pools explained, lending and borrowing in DeFi, risks associated with decentralized finance, and the future potential of blockchain-based financial systems.
