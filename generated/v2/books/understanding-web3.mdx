# Understanding Web3

A beginner-friendly guide designed to help readers understand the next evolution of the internet — a decentralized digital ecosystem powered by blockchain technology, cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, and user-owned digital assets. This book explains what Web3 is, how blockchain technology powers Web3, the role of cryptocurrencies in decentralized systems, what smart contracts do, how decentralized applications function, NFTs and digital ownership, DAOs and community governance, the relationship between Web3 and the Metaverse, and opportunities and risks associated with decentralized systems.
