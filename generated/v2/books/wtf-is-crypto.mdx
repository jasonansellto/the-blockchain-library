# WTF Is Crypto?

Cryptocurrency can feel overwhelming at first. WTF Is Crypto? breaks everything down in simple language — wallets, blockchains, Bitcoin, exchanges, NFTs, and Web3 — designed for everyday readers entering the space for the first time. Readers will learn what cryptocurrency actually is, how blockchain technology works, the difference between centralized and decentralized systems, how to safely use wallets and exchanges, why Bitcoin became important, how Web3 and digital ownership are evolving, common mistakes beginners make, and basic security practices for protecting digital assets.
