# Understanding Blockchain

A beginner-friendly introduction to blockchain technology — explaining decentralized systems, Bitcoin, smart contracts, and why blockchain is becoming one of the most important technological innovations of the modern era. This book breaks down what blockchain technology is, how decentralized networks function, why blockchain was created, the relationship between Bitcoin and blockchain, how transactions are verified, what smart contracts do, how blockchain is being used outside of cryptocurrency, and the role of blockchain in Web3 and digital ownership.
