# Non-Fungible Token (NFT)

A unique digital token that represents ownership of a specific asset. Unlike fungible tokens (where each unit is identical), NFTs are distinct and non-interchangeable. NFTs are commonly used for digital art, collectibles, game items, and representations of real-world assets.

## Definition

A unique digital token that represents ownership of a specific asset. Unlike fungible tokens (where each unit is identical), NFTs are distinct and non-interchangeable. NFTs are commonly used for digital art, collectibles, game items, and representations of real-world assets.
