# Enterprise Blockchain

Blockchain infrastructure designed for organizational requirements such as governance, predictable operations, compliance, and integration.

## Definition

Blockchain infrastructure designed for organizational requirements such as governance, predictable operations, compliance, and integration.

## Why it matters

Enterprise concepts focus on organizational adoption, integration, governance, and operational requirements.

## How it works

Enterprise blockchains use permissioned nodes where only verified participants can record or access data. Smart contracts automate business logic, ensuring transactions only occur when pre-defined conditions are met. Data is encrypted and access is strictly governed by pre-defined roles within the enterprise ecosystem.

## Real-world example

IBM Food Trust is a prominent enterprise blockchain that allows retailers to track food provenance, reducing food safety issues and waste.

## Advantages

- Increased auditability and accountability
- Automation via smart contracts
- Seamless legacy system integration

## Limitations

- Limited public transparency
- High costs of implementation and training
- Regulatory uncertainty in many regions

## Common misconceptions

- All enterprise blockchains are private.
- Enterprise blockchain is just a database with marketing.
