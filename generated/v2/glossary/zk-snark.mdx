# zk-SNARK

A succinct non-interactive proof that can verify knowledge or computation without revealing the underlying witness.

## Definition

A succinct non-interactive proof that can verify knowledge or computation without revealing the underlying witness.

## Why it matters

Zero-knowledge systems enable verification of claims or computation while minimizing disclosure of underlying information.
