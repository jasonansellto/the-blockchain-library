# Vector Smart Gas

The native asset of Vector Smart Chain used for network fees, staking, and other protocol-level functions.

## Definition

The native asset of Vector Smart Chain used for network fees, staking, and other protocol-level functions.

## Why it matters

These terms document Vector Smart Chain concepts in a neutral educational context.

## How it works

Every transaction or code operation requires a specific amount of Vector Smart Gas. The network's protocol calculates this cost based on fixed parameters. Users purchase or hold this gas to pay for their actions, and because the price remains stable, the user knows exactly how much they are spending at any given moment.

## Real-world example

A developer launching a gaming dApp uses Vector Smart Gas to ensure that player transaction costs stay low and consistent during gameplay.

## Advantages

- Ensures stable and predictable dApp costs
- Prevents fee-based price volatility for users
- Facilitates seamless mass-market adoption

## Limitations

- Less flexibility than dynamic fee models
- May require governance updates to adjust prices
- Less responsive to immediate network saturation

## Common misconceptions

- Many think this is just a cryptocurrency token, but its primary function is as a utility for network computation.
- People often assume it will increase in price, but it is explicitly designed to remain stable for network usage.
