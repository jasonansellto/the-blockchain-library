# Proposal

A proposal represents a specific, actionable suggestion submitted within a DAO governance framework to initiate a change or allocate resources. It serves as a technical or strategic document that details the requested modifications to a protocol. A formal proposal typically requires a maturation period, allowing for community debate, technical review, and public feedback. The proposal acts as the 'ballot' that community members vote on, containing the precise data or function calls needed to update the system if approved.

## Definition

A proposal represents a specific, actionable suggestion submitted within a DAO governance framework to initiate a change or allocate resources. It serves as a technical or strategic document that details the requested modifications to a protocol. A formal proposal typically requires a maturation period, allowing for community debate, technical review, and public feedback. The proposal acts as the 'ballot' that community members vote on, containing the precise data or function calls needed to update the system if approved.

## Simple explanation

A proposal is like a formal plan submitted for group approval. If you want a club to spend money on new gear, you write down exactly what to buy, how much it costs, and why it is needed. That document is your proposal.

## Why it matters

It is the starting point for all democratic action in a DAO. Without a clear proposal, there would be no way to track what changes are being considered or to ensure participants know what they are voting on.

## How it works

A proponent initiates a proposal via a governance interface. It includes descriptions and proposed code changes. The community reviews and discusses it until it is ready for an official vote.

## Real-world example

Gitcoin grants proposals suggest funding specific open-source projects based on community donation matching.

## Advantages

- Transparency in intent
- Clear documentation
- Facilitates community debate
- Accountability for action

## Limitations

- Potential for complexity
- Risk of proposal fatigue
- Time-intensive review process

## Common misconceptions

- People often think a proposal is just a message in a forum, but in DAOs, it often requires a blockchain transaction. Some believe all proposals require code changes, but many involve budget or policy changes.
