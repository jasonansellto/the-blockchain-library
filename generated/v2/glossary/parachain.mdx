# Parachain

A Parachain is a custom, project-specific blockchain that runs in parallel within the Polkadot or Kusama relay chain ecosystem. Unlike standalone blockchains that must manage their own security and validator sets, parachains benefit from the shared security and interoperability provided by the central Relay Chain. Parachains are heterogeneous, meaning they can be architected for specific use cases, such as DeFi, identity, or gaming, while maintaining the ability to pass arbitrary data or tokens across other chains within the network.

## Definition

A Parachain is a custom, project-specific blockchain that runs in parallel within the Polkadot or Kusama relay chain ecosystem. Unlike standalone blockchains that must manage their own security and validator sets, parachains benefit from the shared security and interoperability provided by the central Relay Chain. Parachains are heterogeneous, meaning they can be architected for specific use cases, such as DeFi, identity, or gaming, while maintaining the ability to pass arbitrary data or tokens across other chains within the network.

## Simple explanation

Think of the main Polkadot network like a busy city highway. A parachain is like a specialized vehicle driving on that highway. Instead of having to build its own road from scratch, the parachain uses the highway's foundation. Because they all share the same road, they can easily talk to each other and safely travel to different destinations without needing extra permission or bridges.

## Why it matters

Parachains solve the critical blockchain problem of scalability by allowing multiple specialized chains to operate simultaneously without clogging the main network. By enabling seamless communication between different chains, they foster a truly connected Web3 ecosystem where assets and information flow freely across disparate protocols.

## How it works

Parachains submit periodic proofs of their state transitions to the Relay Chain, which verifies these proofs using a group of validators. Once verified, the parachain's state is finalized and secured by the consensus power of the entire Polkadot ecosystem. This shared security model allows parachains to focus entirely on their specific business logic without needing to bootstrap their own decentralized validator network.

## Real-world example

Aeternity or Moonbeam are prominent examples of parachains; Moonbeam, specifically, acts as an Ethereum-compatible smart contract platform on Polkadot, allowing developers to port dApps directly into the Polkadot ecosystem.

## Advantages

- Inherited security from the Relay Chain
- High scalability through parallel processing
- Seamless interoperability with other parachains
- Customizable blockchain architecture and logic

## Limitations

- Expensive acquisition of slots via auctions
- Limited number of available parachain slots
- Dependency on Relay Chain consensus stability

## Common misconceptions

- Many believe a parachain is just a smart contract, but it is actually a fully sovereign blockchain.
- People often think parachains must be identical in design, when they are actually highly customizable for specific use cases.
