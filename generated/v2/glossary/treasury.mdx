# Treasury

A treasury in a DAO is a collective pool of digital assets, including tokens, stablecoins, and NFTs, held and managed by the organization. These funds are locked in smart contracts and are used to finance development, marketing, operational costs, or community grants. The treasury is often the most critical component of a DAO, representing its survival and ability to incentivize contributors. Decisions regarding the allocation of treasury funds are made through the governance process, ensuring that assets are used in alignment with the community’s strategic goals.

## Definition

A treasury in a DAO is a collective pool of digital assets, including tokens, stablecoins, and NFTs, held and managed by the organization. These funds are locked in smart contracts and are used to finance development, marketing, operational costs, or community grants. The treasury is often the most critical component of a DAO, representing its survival and ability to incentivize contributors. Decisions regarding the allocation of treasury funds are made through the governance process, ensuring that assets are used in alignment with the community’s strategic goals.

## Simple explanation

A treasury is the DAO's piggy bank. It stores the money and tokens the project earns or collects. Members vote to decide how to spend this money—like paying developers for new features or giving grants to people helping the project.

## Why it matters

The treasury provides the financial resources necessary for a project to grow and sustain itself. It turns a collection of individuals into a functional organization with real economic power.

## How it works

The treasury is held in a multi-signature wallet or a specialized smart contract. Funds can only be moved or sent if a governance proposal is passed that authorizes the contract to release a specific amount to a specific address.

## Real-world example

The Optimism Collective treasury holds millions in OP tokens to fund public goods and ecosystem projects.

## Advantages

- Collective financial control
- Transparent asset management
- Funding for ecosystem growth
- Prevents individual theft

## Limitations

- Target for hackers
- Requires careful management
- Risk of poor spending decisions

## Common misconceptions

- People often think a treasury is managed by a single person, but it is typically controlled by a multi-sig or governance contract. Some believe it only holds one token.
