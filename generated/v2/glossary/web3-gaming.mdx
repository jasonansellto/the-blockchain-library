# Web3 Gaming

Web3 gaming refers to a new paradigm of video game development that integrates blockchain protocols to decentralize game assets, economies, and governance. By moving beyond centralized servers, Web3 games utilize distributed ledgers to ensure player-owned items, verifiable rarity, and cross-platform interoperability. This approach fosters a community-driven environment where players participate in decision-making via decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), creating a more symbiotic relationship between developers and users.

## Definition

Web3 gaming refers to a new paradigm of video game development that integrates blockchain protocols to decentralize game assets, economies, and governance. By moving beyond centralized servers, Web3 games utilize distributed ledgers to ensure player-owned items, verifiable rarity, and cross-platform interoperability. This approach fosters a community-driven environment where players participate in decision-making via decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), creating a more symbiotic relationship between developers and users.

## Simple explanation

Web3 gaming is the next version of gaming where the players have more power. Instead of the game company owning everything, you own your items, and you can help decide how the game evolves.

## Why it matters

Web3 gaming challenges the current 'walled garden' approach of the gaming industry. It empowers users, introduces decentralized governance, and paves the way for a more open and fair digital economy.

## How it works

Games are built using smart contracts that govern the rules of assets and player interactions. Player assets are stored in decentralized wallets rather than game servers, allowing for true portability across different game clients or marketplaces. Governance tokens grant players voting rights on future updates or treasury allocations via a DAO.

## Real-world example

Illuvium is an open-world fantasy game that integrates DeFi-style staking and community-led governance into its gaming model.

## Advantages

- True digital ownership
- Decentralized governance (DAO)
- Player-driven economies

## Limitations

- Complex user interface
- Scalability challenges for blockchain
- Regulatory uncertainty

## Common misconceptions

- Web3 gaming does not mean the end of fun, traditional game mechanics.
- It is not solely focused on cryptocurrency trading over gameplay.
