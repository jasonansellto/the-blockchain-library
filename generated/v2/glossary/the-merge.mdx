# The Merge

Ethereum's 2022 transition from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake consensus.

## Definition

Ethereum's 2022 transition from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake consensus.

## Why it matters

Ethereum-specific terms explain its execution environment, standards, accounts, and scaling roadmap.

## How it works

The Merge turned off the proof-of-work consensus where miners solved math problems to secure the network. Instead, it enabled the proof-of-stake consensus layer (the Beacon Chain) to become the final authority for all transactions, where validators now reach consensus based on their staked assets.

## Real-world example

Immediately after The Merge, the carbon footprint of the Ethereum network dropped by nearly 100%, effectively silencing criticisms regarding the network's environmental sustainability.

## Advantages

- Over 99% energy reduction
- Improved economic sustainability for ETH
- Foundation for future scalability

## Limitations

- Did not immediately solve gas fees
- Requires long-term validator health
- Complexity introduced new risk vectors

## Common misconceptions

- Many people believe The Merge lowered gas fees, but it was purely a consensus transition and not a scalability upgrade.
- Some thought the transition would cause the network to go offline, but the switch occurred seamlessly without downtime.
