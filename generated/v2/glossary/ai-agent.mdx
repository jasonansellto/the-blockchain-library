# AI Agent

Software that uses artificial intelligence to perceive information, make decisions, and perform actions toward defined objectives.

## Definition

Software that uses artificial intelligence to perceive information, make decisions, and perform actions toward defined objectives.

## Why it matters

AI and blockchain concepts explore verifiable computation, autonomous agents, provenance, and decentralized AI infrastructure.

## How it works

An AI agent functions by integrating an LLM or specialized model with a blockchain adapter. The agent monitors blockchain events, processes the information, and triggers smart contract functions if specific criteria are met. It uses a private key to authenticate its transactions, ensuring the actions are recorded immutably on-chain.

## Real-world example

Fetch.ai agents working in supply chains to negotiate logistics costs and settle payments autonomously.

## Advantages

- Operates 24/7 without human fatigue
- Direct integration with DeFi protocols
- Automated decision-making capability

## Limitations

- Potential for unpredictable automated actions
- High reliance on model accuracy
- Security risks regarding wallet private keys

## Common misconceptions

- People often think AI agents are just chatbots when they are actually active participants in the economy.
- Many assume these agents are centralized systems controlled by one entity.
