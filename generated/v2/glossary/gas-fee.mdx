# Gas Fee

A fee paid to process a transaction or execute a smart contract on a blockchain. Gas fees compensate the network's validators/miners for the computational work of processing transactions. Fees vary with network demand and transaction complexity.

## Definition

A fee paid to process a transaction or execute a smart contract on a blockchain. Gas fees compensate the network's validators/miners for the computational work of processing transactions. Fees vary with network demand and transaction complexity.
