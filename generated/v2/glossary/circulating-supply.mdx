# Circulating Supply

The quantity of a cryptocurrency or token estimated to be actively available in public circulation.

## Definition

The quantity of a cryptocurrency or token estimated to be actively available in public circulation.

## Why it matters

Tokenomics describes how token supply, distribution, incentives, and utility influence a network economy.

## How it works

The figure is derived by taking the total supply of a token and subtracting tokens held in escrow, team vesting wallets, or DAO treasuries. Exchanges and data aggregators monitor blockchain addresses to filter out these non-circulating holdings. As vesting periods end or locked tokens are released, the circulating supply figure updates accordingly to reflect the new market reality.

## Real-world example

Bitcoin has a high circulating supply because the vast majority of its total potential supply has already been mined and is held by the public.

## Advantages

- Better metric for market cap
- Provides accurate market liquidity view
- Helps prevent valuation manipulation

## Limitations

- Subject to estimation errors
- May exclude active staked tokens
- Vesting schedules cause figure fluctuations

## Common misconceptions

- The circulating supply includes every token that will ever exist.
- Changes in circulating supply are always caused by team manipulation.
