# NFT Royalties

NFT royalties are a mechanism embedded within smart contracts that allow original creators to receive a percentage of the sale price whenever their work is resold on the secondary market. This automated system ensures that artists continue to benefit from the increasing value of their work over time, creating a sustainable income model for digital creators. The enforcement of these royalties usually relies on marketplace cooperation, as the smart contract standard defines the royalty, but the exchange must choose to honor it.

## Definition

NFT royalties are a mechanism embedded within smart contracts that allow original creators to receive a percentage of the sale price whenever their work is resold on the secondary market. This automated system ensures that artists continue to benefit from the increasing value of their work over time, creating a sustainable income model for digital creators. The enforcement of these royalties usually relies on marketplace cooperation, as the smart contract standard defines the royalty, but the exchange must choose to honor it.

## Simple explanation

NFT royalties are like a perpetual bonus for an artist. Every time a piece of art is resold from one collector to another, the original artist automatically gets a small cut of the money. It is like a songwriter earning a royalty every time their song is played on the radio, but for digital art.

## Why it matters

Royalties provide a revolutionary way for artists to earn passive income from their work in perpetuity. They change the economics of art by rewarding creators for secondary market success.

## How it works

When an NFT collection is created, the creator sets a royalty percentage in the smart contract (e.g., 5%). When an item is sold on a marketplace, the marketplace software reads the smart contract's metadata to identify the creator's wallet and automatically directs the designated percentage of the sale funds to that address during the transaction settlement.

## Real-world example

An artist releases a digital painting that sells for 1 ETH. Later, a collector resells it for 2 ETH. The artist receives a 10% royalty (0.2 ETH) automatically.

## Advantages

- Provides recurring income for creators
- Rewards artists for asset appreciation
- Automated and transparent payout system

## Limitations

- Not all marketplaces enforce royalties
- Difficult to enforce across multiple chains
- Can be bypassed by private peer-to-peer sales

## Common misconceptions

- People think royalties are guaranteed by the blockchain, but they depend on marketplaces honoring the smart contract.
- Many assume royalties are the same as copyright ownership.
