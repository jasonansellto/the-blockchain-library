# Atomic Swap

An atomic swap is a technical implementation that enables the trustless exchange of one cryptocurrency for another across different blockchains without the need for a centralized exchange. By utilizing Hashed Time-Lock Contracts (HTLCs), atomic swaps ensure that the transaction either completes entirely for both parties or fails completely, preventing one party from stealing the other’s funds. This mechanism relies on cryptographic proofs to guarantee fairness, essentially automating the escrow process through code and eliminating counterparty risk.

## Definition

An atomic swap is a technical implementation that enables the trustless exchange of one cryptocurrency for another across different blockchains without the need for a centralized exchange. By utilizing Hashed Time-Lock Contracts (HTLCs), atomic swaps ensure that the transaction either completes entirely for both parties or fails completely, preventing one party from stealing the other’s funds. This mechanism relies on cryptographic proofs to guarantee fairness, essentially automating the escrow process through code and eliminating counterparty risk.

## Simple explanation

An atomic swap is like a high-tech trade where you and a friend exchange digital stickers, but with a special 'all or nothing' rule. The computer system ensures that either both of you get the other person's sticker, or nobody gets anything at all, so no one can cheat.

## Why it matters

Atomic swaps allow for truly decentralized, peer-to-peer trading. They remove the need for centralized exchanges, which are often targets for hacks and require users to give up custody of their funds.

## How it works

Two parties agree to a swap and create a Hashed Time-Lock Contract. One party deposits their tokens into a contract that can only be unlocked with a secret key. If the second party provides their assets to the contract within a specific timeframe, the first party reveals the secret key to unlock their funds, completing the trade automatically.

## Real-world example

The Komodo platform or the Decred atomic swap implementation.

## Advantages

- Eliminates reliance on centralized exchanges
- Reduces counterparty and custody risk
- Enables cross-chain asset movement

## Limitations

- Requires active participation from both parties
- Limited liquidity compared to centralized order books
- Complex technical setup for average users

## Common misconceptions

- People often think atomic swaps are instant, when they can actually take time due to blockchain block confirmation times.
- Users frequently confuse atomic swaps with cross-chain bridges, failing to realize atomic swaps do not require a third-party validator.
