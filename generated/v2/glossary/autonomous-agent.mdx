# Autonomous Agent

A software agent capable of planning and executing actions with limited direct human control.

## Definition

A software agent capable of planning and executing actions with limited direct human control.

## Why it matters

AI and blockchain concepts explore verifiable computation, autonomous agents, provenance, and decentralized AI infrastructure.

## How it works

These agents use a combination of local perception engines and blockchain-based decision trees. They interface with smart contracts via SDKs that allow them to query data, execute trades, or update state variables. As they process information, they refine their internal logic to better achieve the user's defined objectives.

## Real-world example

Autonolas (OLAS) protocol, which enables the creation of decentralized, autonomous services that run on-chain.

## Advantages

- Total elimination of human bottleneck
- High-speed execution of complex logic
- Continuous adaptive improvement

## Limitations

- Hard to debug complex emergent behavior
- High infrastructure cost for persistent running
- Liability issues in case of error

## Common misconceptions

- People believe autonomous agents are sentient, but they are just sophisticated mathematical models.
- Some think they operate outside of legal regulations, but they remain subject to smart contract rules.
