# TVL

Total Value Locked (TVL) is a metric used to measure the total amount of assets currently being held, staked, or deposited in a decentralized finance protocol. It represents the health, liquidity, and adoption level of a DeFi project. A higher TVL generally indicates greater trust from the community and deeper liquidity, as it suggests that more users are willing to lock their capital into the platform's smart contracts to earn yield or provide market services.

## Definition

Total Value Locked (TVL) is a metric used to measure the total amount of assets currently being held, staked, or deposited in a decentralized finance protocol. It represents the health, liquidity, and adoption level of a DeFi project. A higher TVL generally indicates greater trust from the community and deeper liquidity, as it suggests that more users are willing to lock their capital into the platform's smart contracts to earn yield or provide market services.

## Simple explanation

TVL is like counting all the money sitting in a club's vault. If a club has a lot of money in its vault, people think it's popular and safe to join. It is a way to see how much people trust a specific project with their money.

## Why it matters

TVL is the most common yardstick for evaluating the size and significance of DeFi projects. It helps investors and developers gauge market sentiment and identify which protocols are successfully attracting capital and providing utility.

## How it works

Protocol developers or third-party data aggregators calculate TVL by summing the current market value of all assets deposited in the project's smart contracts. This includes staked tokens, collateral in lending protocols, and assets providing liquidity in trading pools. The number is constantly updated as asset prices and user deposits change.

## Real-world example

DefiLlama provides real-time tracking of TVL across various chains and protocols like Aave, Uniswap, and Lido.

## Advantages

- Indicates protocol adoption and trust
- Provides measure of total system liquidity
- Useful for market trend analysis

## Limitations

- Sensitive to asset price fluctuations
- Can be inflated by incentivized farming
- Doesn't measure long-term viability

## Common misconceptions

- Many think high TVL means zero risk. A protocol with high TVL can still fail due to bad code or malicious exploits.
- Some mistake TVL for market capitalization. TVL measures funds locked in the app; market cap measures the total value of a protocol's governance tokens.
