# Custody

Custody in the cryptocurrency space refers to the storage and management of private keys, which grant the owner control over digital assets. Custodial solutions involve a third party, such as an exchange or a specialized financial institution, holding the assets on behalf of the user. Conversely, self-custody or non-custodial solutions empower the user to hold their own private keys in a secure environment, such as a hardware wallet, placing the full responsibility of security and recovery on the individual.

## Definition

Custody in the cryptocurrency space refers to the storage and management of private keys, which grant the owner control over digital assets. Custodial solutions involve a third party, such as an exchange or a specialized financial institution, holding the assets on behalf of the user. Conversely, self-custody or non-custodial solutions empower the user to hold their own private keys in a secure environment, such as a hardware wallet, placing the full responsibility of security and recovery on the individual.

## Simple explanation

Custody is about who holds the 'key' to your digital safe. If you use a bank to keep your money, they are the 'custodian' of your vault. In crypto, you can let a big company hold your keys for you, which is easier but means you have to trust them. Or, you can hold the keys yourself, which is safer because you are in charge, but if you lose your keys, your money is gone forever.

## Why it matters

Choosing between custodial and non-custodial solutions is the primary trade-off between convenience and absolute control. It determines the user's risk exposure to platform insolvency, hacks, and their own potential for human error in key management.

## How it works

Custody is managed through the safekeeping of private keys, which are long alphanumeric codes that authorize transactions. Custodial services use multi-signature arrangements and cold storage to protect assets from theft, while non-custodial tools like software or hardware wallets provide the user with the seed phrase needed to derive and manage these keys offline.

## Real-world example

Coinbase Institutional providing cold storage custody services compared to a user holding Bitcoin on a Ledger Nano X hardware device.

## Advantages

- User maintains total control
- Mitigates exchange insolvency risk
- Enables true financial sovereignty

## Limitations

- High risk of human error
- Complex recovery processes
- Target for phishing attacks

## Common misconceptions

- Many assume that holding crypto on an exchange is the same as owning it, but you are technically trusting a middleman. People often believe self-custody is only for experts, despite increasing accessibility of user-friendly hardware wallets.
