# Fiat

Fiat currency is legal tender whose value is derived from government decree and economic stability rather than physical commodities like gold or silver. Unlike cryptocurrencies, which rely on decentralized ledgers, fiat is issued and regulated by central banks. It serves as the primary medium of exchange, unit of account, and store of value for modern economies. In the context of blockchain, fiat represents the traditional monetary systems that decentralized protocols aim to bridge, integrate, or, in some extremist cases, replace through digital alternatives.

## Definition

Fiat currency is legal tender whose value is derived from government decree and economic stability rather than physical commodities like gold or silver. Unlike cryptocurrencies, which rely on decentralized ledgers, fiat is issued and regulated by central banks. It serves as the primary medium of exchange, unit of account, and store of value for modern economies. In the context of blockchain, fiat represents the traditional monetary systems that decentralized protocols aim to bridge, integrate, or, in some extremist cases, replace through digital alternatives.

## Simple explanation

Think of fiat as money that your government says has value just because they say so. It isn't backed by a pile of gold in a vault; it is backed by the government's promise and the country's economy. It is like the tokens you use at an arcade: they only have worth because the arcade owner agrees to accept them for games, but here, the 'arcade' is the entire country and its laws.

## Why it matters

Fiat serves as the primary gateway for users entering the cryptocurrency space through centralized exchanges. It acts as the standard unit for measuring the volatility and valuation of digital assets in the global financial market.

## How it works

Central banks control the money supply by adjusting interest rates and authorizing the issuance of new currency units based on macroeconomic policy. This top-down management allows governments to influence inflation, employment, and economic growth. In crypto, digital representations of fiat are often held in escrow by custodians to issue fiat-backed stablecoins.

## Real-world example

The United States Dollar (USD), the Euro (EUR), and the Japanese Yen (JPY) are the most widely used fiat currencies for purchasing assets on platforms like Coinbase or Binance.

## Advantages

- Universal acceptance in global commerce
- Regulated by central banking authorities
- Provides a stable unit of account

## Limitations

- Subject to inflationary monetary policy
- Centralized control and censorship risk
- Cannot be natively programmed on a blockchain

## Common misconceptions

- Many people falsely believe that fiat is still backed by physical gold reserves. It is often incorrectly assumed that fiat currency has intrinsic value independent of government decree.
