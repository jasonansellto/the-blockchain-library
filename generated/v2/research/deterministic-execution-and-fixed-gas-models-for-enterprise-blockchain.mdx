# Deterministic Execution and Fixed Gas Models for Enterprise Blockchain

Authored by Jason Ansell in 2024, this paper explores the critical role of deterministic execution and fixed gas models in the context of enterprise blockchain

## Abstract

Authored by Jason Ansell in 2024, this paper explores the critical role of deterministic execution and fixed gas models in the context of enterprise blockchain solutions. Deterministic execution ensures that a smart contract or transaction produces the same output every time it is run with the same input, regardless of the environment, which is crucial for predictability and auditability in business processes. A fixed gas model, as opposed to variable or unpredictable fee structures, provides cost certainty for enterprises planning and budgeting their blockchain operations. The paper likely details the technical implementations required to achieve these properties, potentially discussing state management, consensus protocols, and virtual machine designs that guarantee predictable resource consumption. By focusing on these aspects, the paper argues for the suitability of blockchain technology in regulated and performance-sensitive enterprise environments where reliability and cost management are paramount.
