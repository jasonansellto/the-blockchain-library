# The Future of DePIN 2024

This report forecasts the trajectory of the DePIN sector into 2025 and beyond, emphasizing the maturation of the tech stack and the influx of traditional hardwa

## Abstract

This report forecasts the trajectory of the DePIN sector into 2025 and beyond, emphasizing the maturation of the tech stack and the influx of traditional hardware capital. The authors analyze the 'bridge' between Web3 and physical reality, noting that the sector is shifting toward enterprise-grade reliability. The paper highlights the potential for DePIN to disrupt traditional telecommunications, cloud storage, and AI compute providers by drastically reducing costs through crowd-sourced infrastructure. It identifies the regulatory environment, specifically regarding hardware standards and data sovereignty, as the primary bottleneck for mass adoption. Through expert interviews and industry data, the report provides a forward-looking strategy for developers and investors, emphasizing the importance of cross-chain compatibility and user-friendly interfaces. The work is significant for its pragmatic outlook on the challenges of competing with established centralized infrastructure giants and its identification of the key technical and economic pivots necessary for long-term scalability.

**Authors:** FutureMoney Group, IoTeX Foundation

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- DePINs are increasingly competitive with centralized infrastructure providers on cost.
- Regulatory compliance is the largest barrier to enterprise adoption.
- Cross-chain interoperability is essential for the future of specialized hardware networks.
- The shift from hobbyist miners to professional operators is accelerating.
