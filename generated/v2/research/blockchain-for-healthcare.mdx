# Blockchain for Healthcare

This official white paper from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) offers a structured perspective on the application of blockchain technolog

## Abstract

This official white paper from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) offers a structured perspective on the application of blockchain technology to address the pressing needs of the modern healthcare industry. It reviews the core features of blockchain, including immutability, transparency, and decentralization, and assesses their suitability for various HHS-relevant domains such as public health data management, provider credentialing, and clinical trial results. The report is purposefully pragmatic, emphasizing the necessity of integrating blockchain with existing health IT systems rather than replacing them entirely. It discusses the regulatory implications and the potential for blockchain to support improved data quality and reduced administrative overhead. This document acts as an authoritative foundational guide for stakeholders in both the public and private sectors, providing a baseline for understanding how blockchain can be utilized to improve the efficiency and efficacy of the U.S. healthcare infrastructure while adhering to established legal and safety standards.

**Authors:** U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2021-01-01

## Key findings

- Blockchain offers the greatest utility in administrative tasks such as provider credentialing and drug supply chain management.
- Integration with existing health IT infrastructure is essential for practical deployment.
- The technology can significantly reduce administrative costs through automated, verifiable record keeping.
- Regulatory alignment is a prerequisite for any widespread public-sector adoption of blockchain.
