# Ethereum tokenomics and token value: a quantitative analysis of on-chain fundamentals (2021–2025)

## Abstract

This quantitative research provides an empirical analysis of Ethereum's tokenomics following the transition to Proof-of-Stake. The Frontiers editorial office compiles on-chain data to assess how fee-burn mechanisms (EIP-1559), staking yields, and layer-2 adoption have affected the value and supply dynamics of Ether. The study utilizes econometric methods to correlate on-chain activity with token price performance over a four-year window. The findings indicate that Ethereum's shift to a deflationary-prone supply model has altered the asset's investment profile, effectively turning it into a yield-bearing 'digital bond' for the network. The paper is significant for its rigorous approach to on-chain fundamental analysis, providing clear evidence of how protocol upgrades and network utility directly influence the underlying economics of a major base-layer blockchain.

**Authors:** Frontiers in Blockchain Editorial Office

**Publication:** Academic Paper

**Publication date:** 2026-01-01

## Key findings

- The EIP-1559 fee-burn mechanism significantly impacts ETH supply dynamics.
- Proof-of-Stake has transformed ETH from a purely speculative asset into a yield-bearing instrument.
- On-chain activity is a robust predictor of long-term token value trends.
- Layer-2 scaling solutions have significantly influenced base-layer demand and supply behavior.
