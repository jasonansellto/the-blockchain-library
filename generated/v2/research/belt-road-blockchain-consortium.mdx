## Abstract

This whitepaper explores the strategic implementation of blockchain technology across the Belt and Road Initiative (BRI). It examines how distributed ledger technology can facilitate cross-border trade, optimize logistics, and improve transparency in international infrastructure projects. The research focuses on the intersection of geopolitical cooperation and decentralized infrastructure. It addresses the challenges of interoperability between disparate national systems and suggests a consortium-based approach to governance to foster trust among participating nations. By providing a framework for digital trade infrastructure, the authors argue that blockchain can reduce friction in international finance and supply chain management, thereby enhancing economic connectivity across participating regions. The paper serves as an introduction to how large-scale government-backed digital initiatives can utilize permissioned ledgers to modernize trade corridors.

**Publication:** Blockchain Research Institute

## Key findings

- Blockchain facilitates transparent cross-border trade infrastructure.
- Consortium models are essential for governing multi-jurisdictional digital projects.
- Distributed ledgers significantly reduce friction in international logistics.
- Interoperability remains the primary hurdle for large-scale economic integration.
