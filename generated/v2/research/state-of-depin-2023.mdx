# State of DePIN 2023

## Abstract

This report captures the pivotal growth phase of DePIN in 2023, documenting the emergence of the term as a unified industry descriptor. It analyzes the early-stage landscape, characterizing the sector by rapid project proliferation and the validation of incentive-based models for hardware distribution. The researchers examine the success and failure patterns of early wireless networks and decentralized compute projects, noting the critical role of community-led expansion. The report provides a comparative study of token emission strategies and the resultant supply-side behavior, offering a historical perspective on the early adoption curve. It highlights the challenges of user acquisition in non-custodial environments and the nascent state of hardware interoperability. As a snapshot of a foundational year, the report serves as a critical historical reference, establishing the baseline against which subsequent years' performance in node growth and network resilience are measured.

**Authors:** Sami Kassab, Salvador Gala

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- 2023 saw the consolidation of various niche fields under the unified DePIN umbrella.
- Community growth is the primary driver for early-stage network coverage.
- Incentive design patterns were heavily influenced by liquidity mining practices.
- Hardware-as-a-service models began to gain significant institutional attention.
