# BSSC General Security and Privacy Standard Version 2

The BSSC General Security and Privacy Standard Version 2 represents a formalized effort by the Blockchain Security Standards Council to establish industry-wide

## Abstract

The BSSC General Security and Privacy Standard Version 2 represents a formalized effort by the Blockchain Security Standards Council to establish industry-wide benchmarks for security and privacy in distributed ledger technologies. Unlike academic papers, this document serves as a normative guide, providing clear requirements for technical architecture, cryptographic implementation, and operational risk management. It covers critical areas such as key management, smart contract development lifecycles, and network security, aiming to harmonize security practices across diverse blockchain platforms. The methodology reflects a consensus-driven approach among experts to define what constitutes 'secure' behavior in a decentralized context. The standard is designed for developers, auditors, and enterprise decision-makers who require actionable guidelines rather than theoretical discourse. It addresses the lifecycle of blockchain assets, focusing on disaster recovery, threat monitoring, and the secure integration of third-party oracles. Its significance lies in its potential to facilitate compliance in highly regulated industries by providing an authoritative framework for risk assessment, thereby reducing the volatility and failure risks associated with insecure smart contract deployments and custody solutions.

**Authors:** Blockchain Security Standards Council

**Publication:** Standard Specification

**Publication date:** 2025-01-01

## Key findings

- Standardization is necessary for enterprise adoption of blockchain technology.
- The document establishes best practices for key management and asset protection.
- Formalizing the smart contract development lifecycle reduces systemic failure risk.
- Privacy compliance is explicitly integrated into the architectural security requirements.
