# Spartan: Efficient and General-Purpose zkSNARKs via Cycles of Elliptic Curves

Spartan represents an advancement in the construction of general-purpose zkSNARKs, focusing on minimizing proof generation costs and improving architectural eff

## Abstract

Spartan represents an advancement in the construction of general-purpose zkSNARKs, focusing on minimizing proof generation costs and improving architectural efficiency. By leveraging the properties of cycles of elliptic curves, the framework allows for more flexible and performant proof systems compared to earlier iterations. The paper outlines a methodology that translates general computation into constraint systems suitable for zero-knowledge proof generation, significantly reducing the overhead associated with large circuit sizes. Spartan is notable for its emphasis on practical performance in real-world blockchain scenarios, where proof generation time is often a critical bottleneck for user experience and network throughput. The research contributes to the broader field of verifiable computing by providing a structured approach to mapping complex logic into proof circuits, thereby enabling more complex smart contract interactions to be verified succinctly and securely without sacrificing general-purpose utility.

**Publication:** Academic Paper

## Key findings

- Introduces a high-performance general-purpose zkSNARK framework.
- Utilizes cyclic elliptic curves to streamline proof construction.
- Reduces computational overhead during the proof generation phase.
- Enhances the practicality of verifiable general-purpose smart contract computation.
