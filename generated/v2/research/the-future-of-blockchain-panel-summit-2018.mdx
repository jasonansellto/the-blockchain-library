## Abstract

This paper summarizes a key industry panel discussion from 2018, providing a comprehensive outlook on the trajectory of blockchain technology. The discussion focuses on the transition from the 'hype' phase of 2017 to the 'real-world utility' phase of 2018 and beyond. Experts discuss the necessary evolution of blockchain from a general-purpose, experimental technology to specialized, enterprise-ready infrastructure. The panel explores the 'blockchain trilemma' and the rise of second-layer scaling, the potential for decentralized finance (DeFi) to disrupt traditional banking, and the role of regulation in shaping global adoption. The consensus among the participants is that while blockchain technology is a foundational shift in how value and data are managed, the timeline for mass adoption will be longer than initial market expectations suggested. The findings emphasize that success will depend on technical breakthroughs in scalability, the development of common standards, and the active involvement of industry incumbents. The paper is a reflective piece on the industry's maturation, highlighting the shift from a 'Bitcoin-centric' view to a broader enterprise-focused approach to blockchain.

**Publication:** Blockchain Research Institute

## Key findings

- Blockchain is shifting from experimental hype to specialized, enterprise-ready use cases.
- Scaling and interoperability are the most critical technical challenges for the future.
- Institutional involvement is required to drive the next phase of mass adoption.
- DeFi and tokenization are identified as key drivers of future financial innovation.
