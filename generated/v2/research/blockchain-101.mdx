# Blockchain 101

## Abstract

This foundational paper serves as an accessible introduction to the core principles of blockchain technology. It explains the mechanics of distributed ledgers, the nature of consensus mechanisms, and the significance of decentralization in eliminating the need for trusted third parties. The document breaks down how blocks are chained via cryptographic hashes and why this architecture creates immutability. It explores the difference between public, private, and hybrid blockchains, and provides real-world examples of where these technologies are being applied. The paper is designed to help professionals without a deep technical background understand the value proposition of blockchain, focusing on concepts like transparency, security, and automation through smart contracts. It frames the technology as a transformative architecture for data management and digital trust, preparing readers for more advanced discussions on specific industrial use cases and the long-term potential of Web3.

**Publication:** Blockchain Research Institute

## Key findings

- Blockchain acts as a decentralized database with immutable properties.
- Consensus mechanisms are the engines of trust in decentralized systems.
- Smart contracts enable the automation of complex business logic.
- Transparency is a byproduct of shared, distributed ledger structures.
