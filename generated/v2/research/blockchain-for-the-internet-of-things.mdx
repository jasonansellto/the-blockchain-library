# Blockchain for the Internet of Things

This whitepaper from TCS offers a business-oriented examination of how blockchain can solve complex data exchange issues within the Internet of Things ecosystem

## Abstract

This whitepaper from TCS offers a business-oriented examination of how blockchain can solve complex data exchange issues within the Internet of Things ecosystem. It explores the transition from traditional, siloed IoT architectures to integrated, blockchain-enabled networks. The paper discusses various use cases, including predictive maintenance, smart manufacturing, and supply chain logistics, highlighting the economic advantages of decentralization. By focusing on trust-based automation through smart contracts, the paper illustrates how businesses can reduce overhead and enhance process transparency. The document is designed for stakeholders looking to understand the practical integration strategies for blockchain within their existing IoT investments, emphasizing the importance of ecosystem-wide collaboration and the standardization of protocols.

**Authors:** TCS

**Publication:** Project Whitepaper

**Publication date:** 2021-01-01

## Key findings

- Blockchain enables automated trust, reducing administrative overhead in industrial operations.
- Smart contracts provide an effective mechanism for automating service-level agreements in IoT networks.
- Transitioning to a decentralized model allows for higher transparency and data sharing across supply chain stakeholders.
- Interoperability between proprietary IoT platforms is a prerequisite for successful blockchain integration.
