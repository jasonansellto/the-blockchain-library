## Abstract

This whitepaper details the architectural design and economic model for the OpenGPU Network, a decentralized marketplace for high-performance computing (HPC) and GPU resources. Addressing the global shortage of compute power for AI and machine learning tasks, the authors propose a protocol that connects GPU providers with developers needing scalable, on-demand compute capacity. The paper outlines the verification process for GPU performance, ensuring that providers meet the technical specifications required for complex AI inference and training. It discusses the integration of a token-based settlement layer that handles real-time payments for compute cycles. By leveraging the abundance of idle GPU capacity, the OpenGPU Network aims to create a lower-cost, permissionless cloud compute market. The document covers the technical implementation, resource discovery algorithms, and incentive mechanisms designed to maintain service quality and reliability within a heterogeneous, decentralized environment, representing a significant contribution to the decentralized compute branch of DePIN.

**Authors:** OpenGPU Team

**Publication:** Project Whitepaper

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- Idle GPU resources can be effectively aggregated into a high-performance decentralized cloud.
- Verification of compute performance is critical for AI-specific workloads.
- Tokenized markets can significantly reduce the overhead of cloud compute provisioning.
- Decentralized compute provides a resilient alternative to centralized providers.
