## Abstract

The 2024 State of DePIN report provides a comprehensive market analysis of the sector's growth, investment trends, and technical adoption. The report tracks the performance of major categories including decentralized storage, wireless connectivity, and compute networks. It presents data-driven insights into supply-side participation, focusing on how token rewards have incentivized hardware rollouts in both urban and rural environments. The analysis examines the maturation of the sector, noting a transition from purely speculative token models to projects focused on real-world utility and integration with traditional telecommunications and infrastructure providers. Significant attention is given to the rise of specialized chains that support DePIN-specific primitives. This report is essential reading for understanding the macroeconomic state of the industry, identifying key industry KPIs such as active nodes, network utilization, and community-driven expansion, while also highlighting the regulatory and technical hurdles that currently face widespread deployment.

**Authors:** Dylan Bane

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2025-01-01

## Key findings

- The sector is shifting toward functional utility and away from pure speculation.
- Specialized L1/L2 chains are emerging to meet the specific throughput needs of DePIN.
- Supply-side participation is increasingly sensitive to token price and hardware ROI.
- Integration with legacy infrastructure is becoming a key differentiator for successful projects.
