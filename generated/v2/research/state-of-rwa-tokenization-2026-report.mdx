## Abstract

The 2026 State of RWA Tokenization Report provides a retrospective look at the industry's growth, identifying the pivotal milestones and macro-trends that defined the year. The report synthesizes data from across the network to provide a clear picture of asset growth, transaction volume, and the geographic distribution of tokenization activity. It examines the shift toward institutional-grade protocols, noting the move from experimental pilot projects to sustained, large-scale financial deployments. Key sections are dedicated to regulatory advancements, focusing on the impact of new legislation on the growth of tokenized credit and real estate. The report includes case studies of major financial institutions entering the space and provides projections for future trends, such as the growth of multi-chain interoperability and the integration of AI-driven asset management with on-chain assets. This report is a vital resource for understanding the current market reality and the future direction of the RWA sector.

**Authors:** Canton Network

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2025-01-01

## Key findings

- 2026 marked a transition from pilot projects to permanent institutional infrastructure.
- Regulatory developments have significantly lowered barriers to entry in major jurisdictions.
- Tokenized private credit has emerged as the fastest-growing sector within RWA.
- Geographic concentration of RWA issuance is shifting toward more crypto-friendly regulatory hubs.
