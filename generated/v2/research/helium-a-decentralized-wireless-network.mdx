# Helium: A Decentralized Wireless Network

The Helium whitepaper outlines the foundational blueprint for a decentralized wireless network, pioneering the concept of incentive-based physical infrastructur

## Abstract

The Helium whitepaper outlines the foundational blueprint for a decentralized wireless network, pioneering the concept of incentive-based physical infrastructure. It introduces the 'Proof of Coverage' consensus mechanism, a novel algorithm that verifies that wireless hotspots are providing genuine radio frequency coverage in their claimed locations. The document describes how the network utilizes a combination of radio hardware and blockchain tokens to create a global, open-source mesh network. By incentivizing individuals to purchase and host hardware, Helium established the viability of the DePIN model for telecommunications. This whitepaper is seminal in the field, detailing the incentive structures, hardware requirements, and the technical architecture that enabled decentralized growth in the ISP domain. It serves as the primary technical reference for the original implementation of decentralized wireless networks and remains a cornerstone for understanding the mechanics of physical-to-digital resource mapping.

**Authors:** Amir Haleem, Andrew Thompson, Shawn Fanning, Sean Carey

**Publication:** Project Whitepaper

**Publication date:** 2018-01-01

## Key findings

- Proof of Coverage creates a trustless mechanism for verifying physical location and connectivity.
- Community-based hardware deployment can successfully replace centralized cell tower rollouts.
- Token incentives drive high-density participation in urban centers.
- Decentralized mesh networks are viable for low-power long-range communication.

## Citation

Amir Haleem, Andrew Thompson, Shawn Fanning, Sean Carey (2018). Helium: A Decentralized Wireless Network. Project Whitepaper. https://whitepaper.helium.com/
