## Abstract

This paper explores the profound impact of blockchain and non-fungible tokens (NFTs) on the creative and intellectual property (IP) sectors. It examines how creators can leverage decentralized ledgers to retain ownership of their content, automate royalty distribution, and directly monetize their work without relying on centralized intermediaries such as record labels, agencies, or digital platforms. The study details the use of smart contracts to embed copyright and licensing terms directly into digital assets, ensuring that creators are fairly compensated for every secondary sale. Furthermore, the report investigates the potential for decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs) to change how creative projects are funded, allowing fans to invest in artists early in their careers and share in their success. The research acknowledges challenges, including the need for robust 'oracle' systems to bridge real-world IP rights with on-chain digital representations, and the current volatility of NFT markets. The findings suggest that while technical and market hurdles exist, the fundamental shift toward creator sovereignty enabled by blockchain is irreversible, marking a transition toward a more equitable 'creator economy' where value creation is more directly aligned with the artist.

**Publication:** Blockchain Research Institute

## Key findings

- Smart contracts enable automated, permanent royalty streams for creators.
- Blockchain reduces the power of centralized intermediaries in copyright management and distribution.
- DAOs provide a new mechanism for fans to fund and govern creative endeavors.
- Bridging physical intellectual property rights with digital tokens remains a legal and technical challenge.
