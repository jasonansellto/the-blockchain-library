# Blockchain Technology Predictions 2024: Transformations in Healthcare, Patient Identity, and Public Health

## Abstract

This report examines the projected evolution of blockchain technology in the healthcare sector, focusing on the convergence of patient identity management, decentralized health records, and public health surveillance. The authors analyze current trends, including the shift toward interoperable, patient-owned data platforms and the potential for blockchain-based public health incentives. The report contextualizes these developments within the broader Web3 and decentralized identity (DID) movements, suggesting that patient identity will become the linchpin for global health record exchange. It provides a strategic look at how blockchain might address long-standing challenges in vaccine distribution, research data sharing, and patient autonomy. By grounding predictions in observed trends, the report serves as a briefing for policy makers and industry leaders looking to understand the transformative potential of blockchain technology in the near to medium-term, emphasizing that the focus is shifting from simple pilots to systemic, infrastructure-level changes in global health.

**Authors:** J. M. K. H. S. R. P. S. K. S., A. K. S.

**Publication:** Technical Report

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- Patient identity management will be the central pillar for the next wave of healthcare blockchain adoption.
- Blockchain is increasingly viewed as a viable infrastructure for global public health initiatives.
- The market is moving from experimental use cases toward systemic integration and interoperability standards.
- Decentralized identifiers are poised to replace traditional, fragmented patient record systems.
