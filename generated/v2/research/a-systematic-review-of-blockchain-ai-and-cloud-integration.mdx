# A Systematic Review of Blockchain, AI, and Cloud Integration

## Abstract

This paper provides a systematic literature review (SLR) covering the tripartite integration of blockchain, AI, and cloud computing. The research examines how these three technologies form a powerful ecosystem, with cloud computing providing the necessary elastic storage and compute, AI providing intelligence and decision-making capabilities, and blockchain ensuring security, integrity, and decentralized coordination. The paper categorizes the academic output into key areas: data security, resource sharing, intelligent smart contracts, and decentralized identity. The study identifies a trend toward 'Serverless AI' on blockchains, where developers deploy AI models that interact with decentralized storage. The findings highlight that while security is greatly enhanced through this integration, complexity in management and interoperability remains high. The authors conclude by proposing a future research agenda focused on energy-efficient protocols and standardized API interfaces that facilitate the interaction between decentralized applications and large-scale cloud-based AI services.

**Authors:** Anonymous

**Publication date:** 2025-01-01

## Key findings

- The synergy between cloud, AI, and blockchain creates a robust foundation for next-generation decentralized apps.
- Data security and integrity are significantly boosted by blockchain in cloud-based AI environments.
- Integration complexity currently limits the widespread deployment of the blockchain-AI-cloud triad.
- Standardized APIs are needed to bridge decentralized applications with centralized cloud-hosted AI models.
