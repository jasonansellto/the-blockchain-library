# Tokenization of Real-World Assets (RWA): A Comprehensive Analysis of Technology Stacks, Platform Architectures, and Comparative Evaluation of Leading Tokenization Platforms

## Abstract

This paper offers a deep dive into the technical architectures underpinning RWA tokenization platforms. The author compares different technology stacks, ranging from permissioned enterprise blockchains to public L2 networks, evaluating their security, scalability, and integration capabilities. The methodology involves a comparative analysis of leading platforms, scoring them on performance metrics and compliance features. A significant portion of the paper is dedicated to the 'Technology Stack' required for RWA, specifically the interplay between smart contract layers, data oracles, and off-chain storage/custody solutions. The study identifies which technical patterns have emerged as industry favorites and why. By dissecting the underlying architecture of major providers, the paper provides a roadmap for developers and enterprise architects to select the right platform based on their specific asset requirements, ensuring both operational efficiency and long-term security in a rapidly evolving ecosystem.

**Authors:** Jung-Hua Liu

**Publication:** Academic Paper

**Publication date:** 2026-01-01

## Key findings

- Platform architecture choices dictate the balance between decentralization and regulatory compliance.
- Public Layer 2 solutions are increasingly favored for their security-to-cost ratio in RWA applications.
- Oracles are the most critical point of failure in current RWA platform architectures.
- Standardized middleware is emerging to abstract the complexity of multiple blockchain environments.
