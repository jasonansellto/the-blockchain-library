## Abstract

This report serves as a high-level overview of the ongoing research initiatives conducted by the Blockchain Research Institute (BRI). It examines diverse enterprise use cases and the practical applications of distributed ledger technology (DLT) across multiple industrial verticals. The methodology involves a synthesis of expert interviews, industry case studies, and comparative analysis of pilot projects currently in development by BRI members. The research emphasizes the transition from theoretical proof-of-concept stages to actual operational deployment in real-world settings. Key thematic areas discussed include asset tracking, inter-organizational trust, and the reduction of friction in complex business processes. The report is significant for stakeholders seeking to understand the current trajectory of blockchain adoption in global enterprise settings, providing a roadmap for future research focus and identifying common barriers such as interoperability and regulatory uncertainty that continue to challenge widespread technological integration.

**Publication:** Blockchain Research Institute

## Key findings

- Enterprise blockchain adoption is shifting from prototype to pilot and production.
- Interoperability between distinct blockchain networks remains a significant hurdle for adoption.
- Organizational culture and governance structures are as critical as technical implementation.
- Trust-less verification mechanisms significantly reduce auditing costs for consortium members.
