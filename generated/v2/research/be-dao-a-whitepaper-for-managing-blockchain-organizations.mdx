# BE DAO: A Whitepaper for Managing Blockchain Organizations

## Abstract

This whitepaper proposes a modular governance framework for the efficient management of blockchain-based organizations. Rather than focusing on theoretical critiques, the author presents a technical and operational blueprint designed to streamline decision-making in high-throughput environments. The proposed architecture emphasizes the use of sub-DAOs, tiered voting thresholds, and automated treasury management to improve organizational agility. The paper details the implementation of a 'Behavior-Enabled' (BE) model, which incorporates reputation scoring alongside stake-weighted voting to favor active contributors over passive holders. This whitepaper serves as both a theoretical proposal and a practical manual, offering concrete guidance on how to structure a DAO to handle scaling challenges while maintaining transparency and decentralization.

**Authors:** C. J. Carswell

**Publication:** Project Whitepaper

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- Modular organizational structures enable DAOs to scale their operations without sacrificing agility.
- Reputation scoring systems provide a more equitable alternative to purely wealth-based voting metrics.
- Automated treasury management protocols significantly reduce administrative overhead in decentralized organizations.
- Tiered voting systems allow for a better balance between community involvement and executive execution.
