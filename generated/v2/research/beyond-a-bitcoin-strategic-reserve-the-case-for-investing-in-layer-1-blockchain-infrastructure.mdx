# Beyond a Bitcoin Strategic Reserve: The Case for Investing in Layer-1 Blockchain Infrastructure

This paper shifts the discourse from Bitcoin as a standalone asset to the underlying utility of Layer-1 (L1) blockchain infrastructure as a critical investment

## Abstract

This paper shifts the discourse from Bitcoin as a standalone asset to the underlying utility of Layer-1 (L1) blockchain infrastructure as a critical investment category. The author analyzes the strategic importance of L1 protocols—the foundational layers upon which decentralized finance, gaming, and digital identity platforms are built—suggesting that these networks represent the 'internet protocols' of the next economic era. The study examines the metrics of L1 health, including network security, developer activity, and ecosystem composability, and argues that institutional capital should view these networks as essential technological utilities rather than merely speculative assets. By providing a framework for evaluating L1 platforms based on their decentralization, scalability, and security trade-offs, the paper offers an investment thesis for professional capital allocators. It concludes that the long-term value of the blockchain ecosystem lies in the sustained growth of these foundational base layers, which will ultimately support a vast landscape of decentralized applications.

**Authors:** David Krause

**Publication:** Academic Paper

**Publication date:** 2024-01-01

## Key findings

- Layer-1 blockchain infrastructure represents a foundational utility comparable to core internet protocols.
- Institutional capital should prioritize L1 ecosystems that demonstrate strong developer activity and composability.
- The value proposition of L1s is tied to their role as the bedrock for decentralized applications and digital economies.
- Evaluating L1 protocols requires balancing the 'trilemma' of decentralization, security, and scalability.
