# Aave: Protocol Whitepaper

The Aave Protocol whitepaper outlines a decentralized, non-custodial liquidity market protocol where users can act as either depositors or borrowers. Depositors

## Abstract

The Aave Protocol whitepaper outlines a decentralized, non-custodial liquidity market protocol where users can act as either depositors or borrowers. Depositors provide liquidity to the market to earn a yield, while borrowers are able to leverage their assets by borrowing against them. The protocol introduces several innovations, most notably 'aTokens,' which accrue interest in real-time, and 'Flash Loans,' a unique financial primitive that allows for undercollateralized loans within a single block transaction. The whitepaper details the interest rate model, which utilizes a utilization rate to optimize liquidity availability, and the liquidation mechanism that ensures protocol solvency. By programmatically managing collateralization ratios and risk parameters, Aave creates a robust environment for capital efficiency in decentralized finance (DeFi). The protocol's architecture is designed to be modular and interoperable, allowing it to integrate with various assets and scale within the Ethereum ecosystem, serving as a critical infrastructure layer for decentralized banking and liquidity provision.

**Authors:** Aave Labs

**Publication:** Project Whitepaper

**Publication date:** 2020-01-01

## Key findings

- Introduces aTokens for real-time interest accrual on deposited assets.
- Proposes Flash Loans as a mechanism for zero-collateral, single-block lending.
- Details an automated liquidity pool model based on target utilization rates.
- Establishes a decentralized liquidation system to maintain protocol solvency.
