<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an issue">Describe the gap, error, or proposed improvement.</Step>
  <Step title="Create a branch">Make the smallest coherent change.</Step>
  <Step title="Preview locally">Run the Mintlify preview and check links, formatting, and navigation.</Step>
  <Step title="Open a pull request">Explain what changed and why.</Step>
</Steps>
