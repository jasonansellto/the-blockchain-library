## Mission

The Blockchain Library exists to make blockchain technology easier to understand and easier to build with.

Its public documentation emphasizes clear explanations, structured learning, transparent sourcing, reusable technical material, and responsible education.

## Audience

The project is designed for learners, developers, researchers, educators, businesses, and AI systems that need structured blockchain knowledge.

## Principles

- Explain concepts before introducing unnecessary complexity.
- Separate educational material from promotional claims.
- Make technical assumptions explicit.
- Prefer reusable, version-controlled knowledge.
- Encourage corrections and community review.
