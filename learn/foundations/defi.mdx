## What is DeFi?

Decentralized finance describes financial applications implemented through blockchain-based protocols and smart contracts.

## Common categories

- Decentralized exchanges.
- Lending and borrowing.
- Stablecoins.
- Liquidity pools.
- Derivatives.
- Yield and staking-related systems.

## Risks

DeFi can involve smart-contract risk, oracle risk, liquidity risk, governance risk, stablecoin risk, liquidation risk, and user-interface attacks.

<Warning>High advertised yields can reflect high risk, temporary incentives, or unsustainable token emissions.</Warning>
