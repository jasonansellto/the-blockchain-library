## Principles

The app should request only the information needed to answer a user's request or operate a documented tool.

Users should never provide private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or authentication secrets.

<Warning>For binding privacy terms, use the current Privacy Policy published by The Blockchain Library or the application publisher.</Warning>
