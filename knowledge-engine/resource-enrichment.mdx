# Enter a resource once, connect it everywhere

Books and research now use canonical URLs and valid expanded-topic IDs.

That allows the Knowledge Engine to surface them automatically from:

- Dynamic catalogs
- Topic relationship views
- Knowledge Explorer
- Coverage reports
- Future search and API responses

## Current enriched resources

The initial enrichment release includes all nine current book objects and the published deterministic-execution research paper.

The next enrichment cycles can add more research objects, tutorials, examples, quizzes, and AI prompts without changing the platform architecture.
