## Enter once, discover everywhere

Books and research now use stable IDs and topic relationships.

A topic hub can surface a relevant book or paper because of structured relationships rather than a hard-coded promotional block.

The initial research object includes **Deterministic Execution and Fixed-Cost Transaction Models in Layer-1 Blockchain Infrastructure**, with its DOI and SSRN identifier stored in one canonical record.
