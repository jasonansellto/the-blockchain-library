## Standards

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Date time-sensitive claims.
- Separate fact from analysis.
- Explain uncertainty.
- Avoid guaranteed financial outcomes.
- Do not request secrets or credentials.
- Update pages when protocols or products materially change.
- Correct errors transparently.

## Educational scope

Content is educational and technical. Readers should independently evaluate legal, financial, tax, and security decisions.
