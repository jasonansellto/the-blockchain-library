The Blockchain Library ChatGPT app

The Blockchain Library ChatGPT app is designed to make blockchain education easier to access through conversation.

Purpose

The app helps users explore blockchain concepts, follow structured learning paths, understand terminology, and locate relevant educational resources.

Current direction

The public experience focuses on educational content. Additional service-connected tools may be introduced after testing, privacy review, cost controls, and reliability work are complete.

Privacy principles

  • Do not request seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or wallet recovery information.
  • Collect only the data required to perform a requested tool action.
  • Explain what a connected tool does before sensitive actions.
  • Use clear retention and support policies.

Documentation plan

This repository will document available tools, expected inputs and outputs, limitations, data handling, and troubleshooting guidance.
