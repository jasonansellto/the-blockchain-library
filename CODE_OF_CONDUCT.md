# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Blockchain Library is committed to a respectful, constructive, and welcoming environment for contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas rather than attack people;
- provide constructive feedback;
- welcome newcomers;
- acknowledge uncertainty;
- correct mistakes openly;
- respect different levels of experience;
- avoid discriminatory or harassing behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment;
- threats;
- discrimination;
- intimidation;
- personal attacks;
- deliberate misinformation;
- abusive language;
- disclosure of private information without permission.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is expected in a technical project.

Strong criticism of ideas is acceptable when it remains evidence-based and respectful.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may:

- request edits;
- moderate discussions;
- remove inappropriate content;
- close disruptive threads;
- restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Concerns should be reported privately to project maintainers when possible.

Reports should be handled respectfully and confidentially to the extent practical.

## Goal

The community should make it easier for people to contribute useful knowledge and harder for poor behavior to distract from that work.
